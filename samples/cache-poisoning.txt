CSC 436 Week 5 — the two mistakes that serve a logged-in page to a stranger
Generated by capture-transcripts.mjs.
==============================================================================

Mistake 1 (origin):  /account is advertised `public, max-age=60` and loses
                     its `Vary: Cookie`.        -> LEAK=1
Mistake 2 (edge):    the cache key does not include the session cookie.
                     -> CACHE_KEY=vary-only or url-only

Neither mistake alone is an incident. Both together is a disclosure.

         LEAK=0  CACHE_KEY=vary-only
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
